## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of AssetDiet and its users seriously. If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability, please report it responsibly:

1. **Do not create a public GitHub issue**.
2. Email your report to security@assetdiet.dev or open a private security advisory through GitHub:
   [https://github.com/092Abdullah/AssetDiet/security/advisories/new](https://github.com/092Abdullah/AssetDiet/security/advisories/new)
3. Please include:
   - A description of the vulnerability.
   - Exact steps or a minimal proof-of-concept to reproduce the issue.
   - The potential impact on users or host systems.

We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you on a coordinated disclosure timeline.
